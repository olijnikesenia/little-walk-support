Little Walk

A little outside.
A lot more you.

Little Walk is a private, self-guided walking journal for iPhone. Choose a mood and a little free time, then follow gentle prompts that help you notice more.

How to use Little Walk

  1. Choose a mood on Way Pulse.
  2. Tap Create My Way and choose 15–120 minutes.
  3. Review or shuffle your walking prompts, then start Flow Mode.
  4. Leave a Mark for a moment you want to remember, or try a small observation detour.
  5. Finish the walk to create a Way Memory and view it in Personal Landscape.

Little Walk provides reflection prompts rather than GPS navigation. You always choose your own streets and surroundings.
